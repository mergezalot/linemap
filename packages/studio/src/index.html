    linemap
    Flowcharts as metro maps · github.com/mergezalot/linemap
    
    
    Keys and gestures
    
    
    
      
    ZoomCtrl+wheel, pinch, + -, double-click
    
      
    Fit0
    
      
    Pandrag the background, or drag with the middle button (also over boxes); wheel or two-finger scroll (Shift+wheel: sideways); arrow keys
    
      
    Highlight a linehover it, or its legend row
    
      
    Pin a lineclick it; Shift-click adds; Esc clears
    
      
    Pin a node's linesclick the node; Shift-click adds
    
      
    Open a node's linkCtrl-click (⌘-click on a Mac)
    
      
    Hide a lineclick its legend row; Alt/Shift-click: only this line
    
      
    Search/ or Ctrl F
    
      
    Next themeT
    
      
    Notes on / offN; off draws the map without them and puts a badge for each in its node: hover it to read the note, click it to keep the note open, click again to close it. Remembered for your next visit
    
      
    SourceS or Source in the toolbar: the DOT text beside the map, in view and edit mode; type in it and the map follows after a short pause (one undo step each; a text that cannot be drawn keeps the last map and marks the line). Drag its edge to widen it; Tab indents, Esc leaves the text, keys typed there are only text
    
      
    EditE, then drag a box to move it, drag a line to bend it, double-click the map for a new box; select a box and drag its + onto another to connect them, select a line and drag its ends to reattach it; Shift+drag the background to select the boxes in a rectangle; click one of several selected boxes again to give them all one size; Align in their toolbar (and the edit bar) lines them up on a side or centre, or spaces them evenly (the outermost two stay), and pins them; their toolbar (and the edit bar) groups them in a new container, merges their containers into the one clicked first, splits them off into a new container, or ungroups a container (click it to select its boxes); a pin marks a pinned box, click it to unpin the box; the toolbar above a selected box adds a note to it (Ctrl Enter adds, Esc cancels; <b> <i> <u> <s> <font color> style its text); Del removes, Ctrl Z undoes. Alt while dragging: off the grid
    
      
    ReloadR
    
      
    Open / pastedrop a file, or paste DOT text anywhere
    
    
    
    
    The address keeps hidden lines, through settings, the theme, notes off and the view, so a link shows the same map.
